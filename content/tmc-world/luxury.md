# TMC LUXURY

## Property Management

TMC Luxury is the operating partner behind a well-managed high-value residence.

### Core Capabilities
- Property Management
- Maintenance & Contractors
- Inspections & Reporting
- Vendor Coordination
- Preparation & Home Readiness
- Additional Property Support

### White-Glove Operating Philosophy
White-Glove is the operating culture: discreet, proactive, highly responsive, anticipatory, accountable, and centered on the owner's experience.

### Integrated Operating Model
- **People** — experienced professionals delivering the service.
- **Estate Management** — proactive oversight, maintenance, inspections, and continuity.
- **Vendor Ecosystem** — trusted contractors, performance oversight, alternatives, and coordinated execution.
- **Operating System** — processes, controls, information, and accountability.
- **Technology + AI** — automation, property intelligence, predictive capabilities, and faster decision support.
- **Owner Experience** — one relationship, one source of visibility, and one team accountable for the experience.

### Owner Outcomes
- Less operational burden
- More time for business, family, and personal priorities
- Confidence that the residence is continuously cared for
- One trusted point of accountability
- Visibility without personally managing the operation
- Proactive handling of needs
- An experience designed around living in the home rather than administrating it.
