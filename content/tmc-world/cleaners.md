# TMC CLEANERS

## Home · Property Care

TMC Cleaners creates immaculate spaces that are ready for the life happening inside them.

### Core Capabilities
- Luxury Home Cleaning
- Airbnb Turnovers
- Recurring Cleaning Services
- Pre-Arrival Preparation
- Post-Stay Care
- Additional Property Support

### More Than Cleaning
Cleaning is the capability. Readiness is the experience.

The objective is to create spaces that feel fresh, cared for, healthy, bright, and ready whenever the client arrives or the property changes from one use to another.

### Property Rhythm
TMC Cleaners adapts its service around owner arrivals, guest check-ins, short-term rental turnovers, important events, post-stay restoration, and recurring care.

### White-Glove Property Care
The TMC standard is expressed through discretion, consistency, attention, responsiveness, and accountability.

### Promise / Signature
Immaculate spaces for a better life.
