# OUR CLIENTS

## Who TMC Is Built Around

People who value their time, their freedom, their privacy, and the way they experience their world.

### Definitions
- Clients may include entrepreneurs, investors, executives, business owners, artists, performers, public figures, property owners, families, and private individuals.
- Clients are defined by how they want to live, not only by wealth, occupation, age, or assets.
- Their worlds may include multiple residences, frequent travel, client-owned assets, people and providers, projects and investments, and demanding schedules.
- Clients value time, freedom, privacy, quality, trust, and simplicity.
- TMC seeks to understand how the client lives, what matters, preferences, priorities, what requires attention, and what is coming next.
- The ecosystem is designed to grow with the client's world.

### Closing
You have the lifestyle. We handle everything around it. More time. More freedom. More life. Your world. Handled.
