# OUR VALUES

## How TMC Operates

The TMC standard lives in how we work.

- **DISCRETION** — Respect what clients trust us with.
- **EXCELLENCE** — Care about the details.
- **TRUST** — Earn it through what you do.
- **TIME** — Respect the most valuable resource.
- **RELATIONSHIPS** — Build something that lasts.
- **POSSIBILITIES** — Always look ahead.

Together, these values create the TMC Standard and guide behavior across all four operating units.
