## THE TMC CULTURE — The human side of TMC

It's not about more things. It's about a better life.

The TMC Culture is the human expression of how the company thinks, works, treats people, builds relationships, and approaches responsibility.

A better life means more freedom to experience life intentionally: family, creation, travel, building, thinking, rest, and enjoyment.

The culture is built by people who understand responsibility, notice details, communicate, act, use discretion, take ownership, and care about results.

Ownership means communicating, coordinating, acting, following through, and closing the loop.

The six cultural values are Discretion, Excellence, Trust, Time, Relationships, and Possibilities.

Anticipation means paying attention, recognizing patterns, understanding context, preparing early, and thinking one step ahead.

Continuous improvement means listening, learning, adapting, and improving while preserving the core principles.

The same culture connects all four operating units.
